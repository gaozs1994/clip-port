## Style Prompt

ClipPort is a calm, capable desktop productivity tool. The video uses a light sea-glass canvas, dark teal ink, strong teal actions, and sparse orange emphasis. Frames feel like a refined extension of the actual app: flat surfaces, crisp dividers, compact labels, and clear progress states. Motion is confident and functional, with directional pushes and controlled focus moves that make the workflow easy to scan.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F4F7F6`
- Ink: `#172220`
- Teal: `#0F766E`
- Mint: `#D9F1ED`
- Orange: `#C2410C`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

- Display and body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, with `Segoe UI` and `Microsoft YaHei UI` fallbacks.
- Data and stage labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, with `Consolas` fallback.

## Motion

- Medium-energy product motion with push-slide transitions as the primary language.
- Use a focus pull for the privacy beat and a restrained scale reveal for the final mark.
- Entrances use varied directions and eases; no jump cuts and no pre-transition exits.

## What NOT to Do

- No cyan or purple neon gradients.
- No glassmorphism, glow-heavy sci-fi styling, or oversized rounded cards.
- No fake browser chrome or invented product features.
- No low-contrast gray text or tiny web-sized typography.
