## Video direction

Build a clean, medium-energy desktop product film. Use the real ClipPort capture as product proof, but place it inside frame-scale compositions with strong Chinese headlines, visible teal actions, and orange stage accents. Primary transitions are horizontal push slides; the trust beat uses a focus pull; the outro settles with a restrained color dip. Keep all copy short and all motion deterministic.
