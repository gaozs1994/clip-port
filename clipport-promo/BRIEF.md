## Intent

Create a concise Chinese promotional video for ClipPort. Lead with the product's core promise, then prove it with the real interface: paste a link, choose a clear preset, and let a transparent, recoverable local queue finish the job.

## Assets

- assets/clipport-icon.svg - official ClipPort app mark for the opening and closing frames.
- assets/screens/clipport-light.png - primary desktop product capture; use as the visual source of truth.
- assets/screens/clipport-dark.png - dark-theme product capture for the theme and continuity beat.
- assets/screens/clipport-mobile.png - responsive capture for the cross-device polish beat.

## Customizations

- Emphasize the three-step workflow, visible processing stages, resumable queue, and local privacy.
- Use short Chinese on-screen copy with no narration.
- Use restrained local UI sound effects; do not depend on online audio services.

## Notes

- Keep all claims grounded in the current PRD and product prototype.
- Match the product's teal, orange, off-white palette and flat, dense dashboard character.
- Avoid generic cyber visuals, neon gradients, fake metrics, or unsupported site claims.
